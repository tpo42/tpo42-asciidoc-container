= UC-004: Build a house toolchain on top of the image
:date: 2026-08-11
:actor-kind: persona
:actor: toolchain owner in a consuming organisation
:level: user goal
:status: needs confirmation

== Goal

Take `tpo42/adoc` as a base and add what a particular organisation needs -- house
fonts, corporate themes, extra gems, an internal Asciidoctor extension -- then
distribute the result internally.

== Main success scenario

. The consumer writes their own Containerfile with `FROM ghcr.io/tpo42/adoc:0`.
. They add their layers and build with their own tooling, into their own engine's
  image store.
. They push to their own registry and consume it exactly as in
  <<uc-002.adoc#,UC-002>> -- devcontainer or compose, referencing their image
  instead of the upstream one.

== What this demands

* A stable, published base image with a predictable tag scheme. The floating
  major (`:0`) is what makes `FROM` viable without re-pinning on every release.
* The image must be useful as a *base*, not only as a finished product: an
  unprivileged user, a documented workspace path, no assumptions that break when
  layers are added on top.
* Nothing from ADCW. The wrappers are not in this path -- the consumer's build
  system is.

== Notes

Recorded because it constrains the *image*, not the wrappers, and because it is
the reason tag stability matters beyond this repository's own CI.

This use case reflects an interpretation that has not yet been confirmed with the
maintainer; the alternative reading is that the intent was extending the running
environment rather than the image. The `:status:` attribute stays until that is
settled.
